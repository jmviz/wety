are not adopted (Proto-Kra's crown, 1967–2909 BP, restates its prior); Tai is only
bounded at 0–2200 years; the root and Southwestern Tai are free. One analysis, not a
consensus. Source review: run `phylogeny-deposits-2026-10-06`.
