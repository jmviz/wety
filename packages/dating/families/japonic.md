# Japonic

**Summary.** Two single-researcher runs (Codex/Astra and Claude, 20 September) plus an editorial correction packet (21 September) that reconciles them: 11 findings revised 12 records. Lee & Hasegawa 2011 give a sampled root of median 2182 YBP (mean 2398, 95% HPD 1239–4190), with no stated epoch (its calibrations imply AD 2010; see below). Robbeets 2021's 2100 bp ±175 is a prior, not an independent confirmation. Huisman et al. 2025 supply further model alternatives (outputs not extracted). Jarosz et al. 2022's AD 800–1300 is a dispersal window within one scenario. 

**Shown in wety (2026-10-06).** Lee & Hasegawa 2011's UCLD + covarion summary tree via
Phlorest (manifest `lee_hasegawa2011_ucld_cov`) reproduces the article's root (median
2182 YBP, 1239–4190); the author's 2018 Zenodo deposit does not (2194, 1232–3847) and
is not used. The article states no epoch but equates 407 YBP with the Tokugawa
regime's establishment in Edo (1603), so ages are read before AD 2010. `jpx-pro` ends
at the root (Ryukyuan against mainland incl. Old Japanese), 2181 BCE – 771 CE;
`jpx-ryu-pro` at the Ryukyuan crown (Amami–Okinawa and Miyako–Yaeyama–Yonaguni,
support 0.98), 82 BCE – 1694 CE. One analysis, conditional on its calibrations.
