# Japonic

**Summary.** Two single-researcher runs (Codex/Astra and Claude, 20 September) plus an editorial correction packet (21 September) that reconciles them: 11 findings revised 12 records. Lee & Hasegawa 2011 give a sampled root of median 2182 YBP (mean 2398, 95% HPD 1239–4190), with the epoch not established in the text read. Robbeets 2021's 2100 bp ±175 is a prior, not an independent confirmation. Huisman et al. 2025 supply further model alternatives (outputs not extracted). Jarosz et al. 2022's AD 800–1300 is a dispersal window within one scenario. Proposals are unimported; no sampled clade is mapped to a Wiktionary stage.

**Start here:** [`japonic-stocktake-2026-09-21/README.md`](../../../data/workspaces/dating/_research/japonic-stocktake-2026-09-21/README.md) is the current reading, with the combined source inventory, corrected synthesis, and three Sino-Japanese borrowing candidates from Pellard 2015 (`*bau`, `*sjaugwatu`, `*zjauzu`) as a possible etymology-level application.

Original runs (immutable): `single-frontier-japonic-2026-09-20-v2/` (Astra) and `single-frontier-japonic-claude-2026-09-20-v2/` (Claude) under `data/workspaces/dating/_research/`. Use the `*-targeted-revisions.jsonl` files in the correction packet in place of the original records they supersede.
