# Semitic

**Shown in wety (2026-10-06).** Kitchen et al. 2009 (25 languages, binary standard
model, relaxed clock), summary tree redeposited by Phlorest (manifest
`kitchen2009_semitic_mcc`): root mean 5757, 95% HPD 4383–7338, against the article's
5750 YBP (4400–7400). The approximate match, and a redeposit rather than an author
deposit, are noted on the decisions. YBP has no stated epoch, so it is read as AD 1950
by convention (labeled). Calibrations are five dated extinct tips (Akkadian 2800,
Ugaritic 3400, ancient Hebrew 2600, Biblical Aramaic 1800, Ge'ez 1700 YBP).
