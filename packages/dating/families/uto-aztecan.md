# Uto-Aztecan

**Shown in wety (2026-10-06).** Greenhill, Haynie et al. 2023 (34 varieties plus the
Kiowa and Tewa outgroups; covarion relaxed clock), the authors' MCC tree (manifest
`greenhill2023_ua_covarion_relaxed`). `azc-pro` ends at the 34-language crown,
2974–1308 BCE (MCC node, support 0.81, 3257–4923 BP). The article's 4,095 BP
(3,258–5,025) is the MRCA time over all posterior trees (the deposited log summary
gives 4094.8, 3258.2–5025.1), so the two differ in their upper bound. BP has no stated
epoch; the Classical Nahuatl tip (445 BP) is tied to both 1555 and 1571, so the 1950
convention is used (labeled).

Not adopted: Proto-Nahuan and Proto-Numic. Both nodes carry archaeological priors
(Nahuan 1,570–2,410, Numic 1,290–1,800 years) and their posteriors stay within them.
