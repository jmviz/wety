# Dravidian

**Shown in wety (2026-10-06).** Kolipakam et al. 2018's main model (cov-est-relax,
20 languages; annotated MCC tree from the journal's Figshare supplement, manifest
`kolipakam2018_cov_est_relax`) reproduces SI Table 2: root median 4433, 95% HPD
2812–7063 years. The source writes "years ago" without an epoch; its calibrations
equate 254 BCE with 2250 years ago and c. 700 CE with 1300, so ages are read before
AD 2000. Four proto ends, 95% HPD:

| Code | Clade (sampled) | End |
|---|---|---|
| `dra-pro` | root: North, Central, South-Central, South | 5064–813 BCE |
| `dra-sdo-pro` | South I, incl. Tulu | 1735–251 BCE |
| `dra-sdt-pro` | Telugu + Gondi, Koya, Kuwi | 1941 BCE – 599 CE |
| `dra-nor-pro` | Brahui + Kurukh–Malto | 251 BCE – 729 CE |

South I, South II and North are constrained monophyletic; South I must be at least
2250 years old and the Brahui branch under 2250, so one end of each of those HPDs is a
prior bound. Not adopted: Central and South Dravidian (not clades in this tree).
Other models in SI Table 2 give overlapping root intervals; one analysis, not a
consensus. Source review: run `phylogeny-deposits-2026-10-05`.
