# Bantu

**Shown in wety (2026-10-06).** Grollemund et al. 2015 (424 Bantu and related
languages), summary tree via Phlorest (manifest `grollemund2015_bantu_mcc`), whose
means reproduce the article: root with the Grassfields and Tiv outgroups 6,929.7 B.P.,
Bantu in-group 4,846.5 B.P. B.P. is read as AD 1950 by convention (labeled).

`bnt-pro` ends at the in-group's crown, 416 languages (Guthrie zones A–S and
Jarawan): 3051–2624 BCE (the deposit's 95% HPD; the article gives ± 138.1). The
authors' calibration b bounds this node to 4,000–5,000 B.P., so the interval largely
reflects that prior. The outgroup root is not adopted for Proto-Bantoid, which has
many unsampled branches.
