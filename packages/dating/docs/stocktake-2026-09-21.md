# Dating stocktake, 21 September 2026

The project can now conduct bounded autonomous family research and retain useful,
auditable proposals. The immediate bottleneck is turning those proposals into
faithful source assertions, especially in synthesis and relative chronology. Another
backend matrix or harness redesign is not the next milestone.

It is not the current count after later source-packet work. These stages should not

## Work completed in this continuation

A bounded coordinator review re-read retained contexts for identified problems.
It produced **11 findings and targeted revisions to 12 records** (10 Claude, 2
Astra), plus report corrections. This is development review, not an independent
gold standard or comprehensive scoring of the 42 records. Originals remain intact.
The [review packet](../../../data/workspaces/dating/_research/japonic-stocktake-2026-09-21/README.md)
contains the corrected synthesis, per-finding evidence offsets/hashes and revised
proposal files. The numbers are not comparable backend error rates.

The consequential corrections are:

- An inferred contemporary BP epoch is not source-stated “years before publication.”
  Lee–Hasegawa's normal-prior parameters and reported interval also conflict; preserve
  the conflict rather than silently repairing it.
- A node can have both a calibration prior and a posterior. Finding the prior does
  not establish that a posterior is absent or identify which earlier study supplied it.
- The Japonic root split and internal Ryukyuan diversification are different nodes;
  dispersal is a third event.
- Contact lasting until a date does not date every borrowing after that date.
  “Not older than the fourteenth century” permits that century itself. A paragraph
  aggregating subfamilies does not distribute every lexical example to every family.
- A self-audit incorrectly described a PDF line break. Exact substring matching
  accepted a fragment without the date; neither check guaranteed adequate evidence.
- Astra's structured slash-century “alternatives” overcommitted beyond its careful
  prose. The corrected representation retains the ordinals without choosing a
  disjunction or excluding intervening time.
- Claude missed a relevant 2025 study acquired in the Astra run. Conclusions must
  describe the searched literature, not claim literature-wide uniqueness.

Only demonstrated engineering issues were changed: Claude counts distinct assistant
message IDs instead of repeated thinking/text/tool frames; unidentified frames and
terminal counts remain conservative. OpenAlex responses now omit large nested
metadata while retaining acquisition URLs, omission counts and the full raw response.
Status calls return compact source handles rather than repeating all headings and
attachments. The research brief incorporates the source-semantic lessons above.
These changes are offline-tested; no post-change live quality gain is claimed.

## Operating decisions and next steps

1. **Keep one subscription frontier researcher as default.** Codex/Astra high remains
   the chosen baseline, Claude/Opus high a demonstrated alternative. High is provisional,
   not an optimized setting. Neither increasing effort nor a reviewer chain has yet
   shown enough benefit to justify another comparison campaign. OpenRouter remains
   explicit paid routing; the new single-agent transport has not had a live trial.
2. **Reuse acquired evidence before buying another family run.** The combined Japonic
   inventory has six substantive works; the two runs fill some of each other's gaps.
   The correction packet is now the starting point, not a third Japonic discovery run.
   Exact sample lists, per-model posterior outputs and the critique/reply chain remain
   specific optional follow-ups. Unknowns do not justify restarting the dossier.
3. **Keep the research useful to wety.** The retained Pellard
   passage supplies three named loan candidates and conditional innovation-based
   ordering. The packet records their premises and withheld edge mappings. Before
   import, identify the actual donor/recipient lexeme nodes and represent the source's
   ambiguous century expressions faithfully. Do not import a simplified contact date
   as a constraint on every word. This optional alignment work does not block
   family expansion.
4. **For expansion, run one new family once.** Use the documented single-frontier
   prepare/run/check commands, 900 seconds research plus 300 delivery as in the pilots,
   and the existing action caps. The next default is **Turkic**, as substantive
   proposal research, not a held-out benchmark: it already appears in retained
   macro-family literature. Reserve recent-work and critique searches. Finish with
   one source-based correction pass on consequential assertions and report its cost
   separately. No repeated family runs until the prose looks satisfactory; no mass
   ingestion. Proposal delivery is a successful endpoint even when import is withheld.
5. **Keep the remaining engineering queue small.** Reliable accounting across partial
   subscription attempts, assertion-level supplemental evidence, and an optional
   deterministic audit of self-check ID/span coverage are useful candidates. They
   should be driven by the next delivered packet, not prerequisites for it. The full
   pilot sensitivity/held-out agenda remains scientific backlog, not a blocker on
   bounded proposal research.
